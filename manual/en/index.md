# Amalino user manual

Amalino is a free app that speaks for children who can't rely on speech. The child taps pictures and words, and the device says them out loud. This manual is for the family and for everyone else who talks with the child.

You don't need to read it all at once. Start with **Getting started**, then come back when you want to change something.

4. [Quick phrases and More](quick-phrases.md): stop, help me, the family's own phrases and the Favourites folder.
5. [The keyboard](keyboard.md): typing, word suggestions and key sizes.

11. [Tips for families](tips-for-families.md): modelling and small daily steps.
12. [Privacy](privacy.md): what stays on the device and what is downloaded.
