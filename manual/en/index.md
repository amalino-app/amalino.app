# Amalino user manual

How to use Amalino, the free AAC app for children. The manual is being written; each page says so until it is ready.

## Contents

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): Installing Amalino and the first setup: the child, languages, voice, board size and touch.
2. [The child's board](the-board.md): What the child sees: the board, the message bar, speaking a sentence, the bars and buttons.
3. [Words and folders](words-and-folders.md): Core words, folders, hidden words and levels, revealing new words over time.
4. [Quick phrases and More](quick-phrases.md): The phrase row (stop, help me, the two family phrases), Favourites and the More page.
5. [The keyboard](keyboard.md): Typing on the board: layouts, word suggestions and key sizes on phones.
6. [Scenes](scenes.md): Photo scenes: taking a photo, drawing areas that speak, showing words, placing the scene on the board.
7. [Editing and moving buttons](editing.md): Grown-up mode, changing a button, adding words, moving a button, undo.
8. [Settings, screen by screen](settings.md): Every settings screen and what each choice does.
9. [Voices](voices.md): Choosing a voice, natural voices, the child's name and how words are spoken.
10. [Sharing and printing](sharing-and-printing.md): Backups, moving to a new device, printing boards.
11. [Tips for families](tips-for-families.md): Modelling, small steps, and using Amalino every day.
12. [Privacy](privacy.md): What stays on the device, what is downloaded, and why.
