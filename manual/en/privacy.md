# Privacy

Amalino is built so the child's words stay with the child. There are no ads, no tracking and no account.

## What stays on the device

- The child's boards, words, photos and recordings.
- What the child says, and the usage logs behind **Progress** and its **This week** summary.
- The child's name.

They're stored only on the device, encrypted. The daily automatic backup stays on the device too. A backup you export is a file protected by your password; where you keep it is up to you.

## What Amalino downloads

Some things are downloaded once, when you ask for them, and then work offline:

- **Voices**: the natural voices and the voices for one language. Amalino tells you the size first, and asks before using mobile data.
- **The speech recogniser** for **Partner listening**.
- **Pictures**: if you search all of ARASAAC or other symbol sets online, only your search words are sent, nothing about the child. Pictures you choose are saved on the device.
- **Offline translation**, where the device supports it: the language is downloaded once. No words from the app are sent.

## Features that work on the device

- **Partner listening** recognises speech on the device. Audio is never saved or sent.
- **Pages for the moment** can use the time, a saved place or today's calendar event titles. All of it is checked on the device only and never uploaded.
- **Suggest hotspots** looks at a scene's photo on the device. On Android it uses Google ML Kit on the device: photos never leave it, but ML Kit sends Google anonymous usage and performance information. iPhone and iPad use Apple's own on-device recognition.

## What could leave the device

Only what you choose to send:

- a backup file you export and share,
- boards you export to another app,
- a printed or saved PDF.

**Sync and sharing**, the **Board library** and the cloud helpers would use an online service. Where this version doesn't have one, their screens say so, and nothing is sent.

## Deleting everything

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. Keep an exported backup if you want to restore later.
