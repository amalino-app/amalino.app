# Settings, screen by screen

Hold the lock at the top of the board for 3 seconds and answer the sum to open the grown-up controls. They're in four groups. This page goes through them in the order you see them.

Most screens have a short tip, **Why this matters**.

## Using it together

### Modelling mode

Tap words while you talk, to show the child how. Your taps don't count as the child's words. A line at the top says "Modelling: you are showing, the child is watching", and hidden words show dimmed, so you can model them too. **Show me where** finds a word for you. Tap **Done** to leave. See [Tips for families](tips-for-families.md).

### Show me where

Type a word to see the path to it, button by button.

### Daily coaching

One 60-second modelling tip a day, and the **Word of the week**: up to 3 words the grown-ups model this week, highlighted in modelling mode. **Daily reminder** sends a notification at the time you choose; it asks for permission only when you turn it on.

### Progress, Weekly summary and Goals

- **Progress**: new words, word combinations and the most-used words.
- **Weekly summary**: a short text about the week to share with everyone who talks with the child, with one word to model next.
- **Goals**: a weekly target, such as days used, new words or words per sentence, with progress.

Use the numbers to choose what to model next, not to test the child.

### Why this helps

Short answers to common worries ("Will this stop my child from learning to talk?"), with the research.

## Settings

### The child's name

The name, and how it's written in each language. You can also set how it's spoken. See [Voices](voices.md).

### Speech and voices

The voice for each language, speaking speed, voice age, volume and saved speech. See [Voices](voices.md).

### Touch and access

How the board reacts to touch. Change one thing at a time and watch the child for a few days.

- **Hold time**: the finger must stay on a button this long before it speaks. Helps when the hand brushes the screen, but a quick tap won't work.
- **Ignore repeated taps within**: a second tap on the same button soon after is ignored.
- **Activate when the finger lifts**: the child can slide to the right button before it counts.
- **Bigger touch area**: each button reacts a little beyond its edge.
- **Tremor filter**: for shaky hands, the button that counts is **Where the finger first lands** or **Where the finger rests longest**.
- **Ignore a resting hand**: a palm or a hand resting on the screen doesn't press buttons.
- **Highlight while speaking**: a soft tint on the button while its word plays.
- **Practise with the child**: a practice board that counts taps, ignored touches and misses between buttons.

Other ways of choosing are listed as **Coming in a later version**.

### Display, hearing and sensory

- **Look**: **Standard**, **Calm**, **Dark**, **High contrast**, or **Teen (no childish look)**.
- **Word-type colours**: **Standard (Fitzgerald key)**, **Calm colours**, **Colour-blind safe** or **No colours**. **Colour as border only** gives a calmer look.
- **Show words under pictures**, **Label above the picture**, **Text size**, and **Space between buttons**.
- **Reduce motion**: follows the device's setting, or always on or off.
- **Mute interface sounds**.
- **Show quick phrases**: the phrase row at the bottom.
- **Early mode**: very simple, big buttons that speak at once, with no message bar. The full vocabulary stays underneath.
- **Vocabulary level**: see [Words and folders](words-and-folders.md).
- **Board orientation on this phone**: phones only. The board, the keyboard and scenes stay upright (or sideways); grown-up screens still turn. If the other orientation fits fewer buttons, Amalino asks before changing.
- **Grid size**: see [Words and folders](words-and-folders.md). On a tablet, a note says when some long words won't fit in full at 84 buttons upright.

### Message bar

- **Show the message bar**.
- **Speak each word when tapped**: on by default. When off, words only go into the message until it's tapped.
- **Clear the message after speaking it**.
- **Word forms**: hold a word to choose another form (dogs, went, bigger…).
- **Log adult modelling separately**: counts how often adults model, never mixed into the child's words.

### Keyboard

**Word suggestions**, **Learn from what your child types**, **Layout** and **Key size on this phone**. See [The keyboard](keyboard.md).

### AI helpers

All off by default; the child always chooses. **AI helpers on** switches them all on or off. They run on the device:

- **Highlight likely next words**: gently outlines 2–3 buttons. Buttons never move.
- **Sentence from first letters**: type "iwgp" to be offered "I want to go to the park".
- **Suggest pages for the moment**: lunch at noon, the park when you're there. A card the child can ignore.
- **Partner listening**: while you switch the microphone on in the top bar, the device listens to what you say and highlights words that could answer. Speech is recognised on the device; audio is never saved or sent. It needs a one-time download.

### Pages for the moment

The rules behind **Suggest pages for the moment**: a page at a time of day, at a saved place, for a calendar event (by a word in its title), or at meal times. Places and calendar events are checked on the device only.

### Keep the child in the app

Step-by-step instructions for **Guided Access** on iPad and iPhone, and **app pinning** on Android (on Samsung tablets also Samsung Kids). The child can still talk; they just can't leave the app.

### Edit lock

- **Simple sum (default)** or **PIN code** (4–8 digits). The PIN is for the device: the same PIN opens the grown-up controls for every child on it. Depending on the device, a PIN needs a screen lock or a recovery code you write down, so a forgotten PIN can be reset.
- **Hold time to open**: how long the lock must be held.
- **Lock again after**: how soon the grown-up controls lock themselves.
