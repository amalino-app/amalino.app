# Quick phrases and More

Some things need to be said fast, without building a sentence. The **phrase row** at the bottom of the board keeps them one tap away, on every page.

<!-- screenshot: the phrase row -->

## The phrase row

From left to right:

1. **★ Favourites**: opens the Favourites folder.
2. **! stop**
3. **! help me**
4. **First family phrase**
5. **Second family phrase**
6. **More**: opens the page with all the phrases.

Every button keeps its place, in every language and on every page. **Stop** and **help me** are always there and always red: they're the urgent phrases. The four phrase buttons are all the same width, so a long phrase never pushes anything aside; it wraps onto a second line instead.

A tap on a phrase says it straight away. It doesn't go into the message bar.

## Choosing the two family phrases

Open the grown-up controls and choose **Quick phrases**. Under **On the board**, pick the **First phrase** and the **Second phrase** for the slots next to stop and help me. Choose the two the child needs most, for example "I need a break" and "I don't like it".

## The More page

**More** opens a page of up to 12 phrases, each in its own fixed place. A tap says the phrase and closes the page; **Close** goes back without saying anything.

In **Quick phrases**, under **Phrase page**, you can:

- **Add** a phrase and choose its **Place on the page**,
- **Edit** a phrase, or mark it **Urgent** so it shows in red,
- **Delete** a phrase. Its place stays empty; the others don't move.

When the page is full, remove a phrase, or tap **Make a phrase board** to turn the phrases into a folder with room for more.

Phrases with the child's name in them, like "My name is…", appear once you've added the name.

## Favourites

The **Favourites** folder holds the things the child loves most: the five favourite things from setup and anything you add later. It's one tap away from every page through **★ Favourites**.

The main board keeps one place free for Favourites too, marked "Kept free for ★ Favourites" when you choose where a new button goes.

On small phones, the ★ button may show the star without the word. It does the same thing.

## Hiding the phrase row

If the child is distracted by the row, you can turn it off in **Settings → Display, hearing and sensory → Show quick phrases**. Think twice: stop and help me are often the most important words a child has.

> **Tip:** Model "stop" and "help me" in calm moments too, not only when things go wrong.
